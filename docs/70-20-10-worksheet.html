⚠️ Check Yourself

If your allocation feels like 30-50-20, that's your burnout signal.

Here's what that looks like:

  • You're skipping breaks to "maximize the event"
  • You said yes to three panels instead of one
  • Your "stretch" activities are actually terrifying
  • You have zero solo time on your calendar
  • You're telling yourself "I'll recover when I get home"

Reset: Move things back. Protect your 70%. A conference where you show up as your best self for 3 conversations beats one where you're a zombie for 30.

Citation: Based on the Center for Creative Leadership's 70-20-10 learning framework from their "Lessons of Experience" research (1980s). Originally developed to describe how leaders learn (70% experience, 20% relationships, 10% formal training), adapted here as an energy management model for introverted professionals navigating high-stimulus environments.
